Things I know about myself:

I work best in the first two hours after I wake up.
After lunch I am slow, so I keep my meetings there.
I write every plan down before I start it.
If a plan is not written, I do not trust it.

I say yes too fast when someone I respect asks for help.
Later I resent the extra work, and I hate that I do.
I am trying to wait one day before I agree to anything.
So far the waiting has saved me two weekends.

My closest friend is Priya Nair, and she tells me the truth.
When we argue, she wins because she listens longer.
I still email her at priya.nair@example.com every Sunday.

I prefer a written message to a surprise call.
A call without warning makes me feel cornered.
If it is urgent, I would rather get a text first.
Then I can call back once I have my notes.

I learn by building small things and breaking them.
Reading the manual first bores me.
I go back to the manual after the first mistake.
That order has worked for every tool I use.

Money makes me nervous, so I track every expense.
I keep three months of rent aside and never touch it.
Spending on books does not count, in my rules.
Spending on gadgets counts double.

I want work where I can see who it helps.
Titles do not matter much to me.
I left a job once because nobody used what we made.
I still think about that team.

On bad days I walk until I can name what is wrong.
Usually it is one sentence someone said.
Writing that sentence down makes it smaller.
Then I can sleep.
