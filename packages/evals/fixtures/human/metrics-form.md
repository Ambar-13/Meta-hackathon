# Human metrics form (A6)

Ask each person in a real pair privately, after they have read their own brief and before they talk to each other about it. Write the answers into `human/responses/<pairId>.json` with sides only, never names.

Before the run, write one sentence that defines the outcome you will judge the brief against, and keep it: `outcomeDefinition` in the file.

1. Did the card represent you? Answer 1 to 5, where 1 is "not me at all" and 5 is "that is me".
2. Did any brief line feel unsupported? Which? Copy the line text or write "none".
3. Did the brief surface something worth discussing? Answer 1 to 5.
4. Would you use it before choosing a teammate? Yes or no, and one line why.

For known collaborators only: list every friction point the brief raised, and for each mark whether both of you knew it before the reveal (`knownBeforeReveal: true`) or not. A friction point you both knew is not a discovery; the table counts both.

Do not ask about a compatibility score or whether the relationship will work; the product makes no such claim (docs/known-limits.md).
